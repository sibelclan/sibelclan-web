Sibelclan is an alternative rock band from Granada, Spain, with Stoner and Grunge influences. The line-up is Juan Luis Manosalbas (vocals), Jorge “Boke” Campaña (drums), Pedro Pardal (guitar and backing vocals) and Isra Jiménez (bass). The band was born in 2013 when Gerardo Hernández and Juan Luis Manosalbas joined forces on a shared project that produced original songs of great quality, with a sound unlike anything the local scene had offered so far. The rest of the line-up came together after several comings and goings, until it settled into the current one.

Their live presence and sound is the main course of this Granada quartet, destined to become a cult band in the rock scene. After a debut album, “Half Marathon”, recorded without a label at Villalobos Estudio and released in 2016, Sibelclan is the talk of Granada's most devoted rock fans, and the limited edition of the album, just 500 copies, is becoming a collector's item.

The band has ten music videos, and its albums are available on Spotify and other digital platforms. In 2022 Sibelclan was selected from more than a hundred bands from all over Spain and, after several rounds, won the Rock in Rio Tea band contest, held in Ponteareas (Pontevedra).

That same year they recorded their second release, the EP “Full Marathon”, which they presented live at Discos Bora-Bora on 10 February 2023 together with the music video for the single “Run to Me”.

In December 2024 they released “Addicted”, produced by Luis Loyo, which was reviewed by Metal Hammer España, El Mundo de Tulsa and Rockemotion.

On 2 October 2026 they released “The Siren Song”, their latest single, again produced by Luis Loyo, with a music video shot as an end-of-summer pool party together with the band's fans and friends. Sibelclan is also one of the 8 finalist bands at BlackSoundFest, whose final takes place on 17 October 2026 at Teatro CajaGranada in Granada.
