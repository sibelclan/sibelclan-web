# Cookie policy

## What they are

Small files that the site or its providers store on your device to remember preferences, measure usage or show advertising.

## How to manage them

When you arrive you will see a banner where you can accept the use of cookies. You can change your decision by deleting the site's cookies in your browser. Technical cookies do not require consent.

## Cookies used by the site

| Provider | Type | Purpose | Consent required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Osano Cookie Consent | Technical | Remember your cookie choice | No |
| Google Tag Manager | Analytics | Load and manage measurement tags | Yes |
| Microsoft Clarity | Analytics | Heatmaps and anonymous usage recordings | Yes |
| Metricool | Analytics | Traffic and social media measurement | Yes |
| Meta Pixel | Advertising | Measure and optimise Facebook and Instagram campaigns | Yes |
| YouTube, Spotify, Bandsintown | Third-party | Play or display their embedded content | Yes |
